Hand-wash · BA winter 79–80% humidity · 48–72hr drying
LAUNDRY SYSTEM
BA winter June–July = 79–80% relative humidity. Clothes hung indoors take 48–72 hrs minimum. Thick items closer to 72 hrs. Never wash something the night before you need it.
Full hand-wash protocol (~65 min active)
  • Pretreatment (20 min passive): H₂O₂ + baking soda paste on armpits, collar, cuffs. Leave 20 min while doing Anki or reading.
  • Pre-soak (15 min passive): Cold water + soap in bucket. Submerge items. Use time for something else.
  • Wash (30 min active): Scrub each item thoroughly. Rinse twice until no suds.
  • Wring + hang (15 min): Roll in dry towel first (removes 30% more water). Hang spread out, never overlapping. Near window or heater.
  • Best drying time: Hang near window 11:00–14:00 when BA winter sun warms the room.
